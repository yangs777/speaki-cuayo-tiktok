Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Speaki Cuayo Publisher is a private creator tool used by the Speaki Cuayo channel owner to prepare and publish short-form videos to TikTok.

Information used

When authorized with TikTok, the tool may use basic TikTok account information needed for authentication and the permissions required to upload or publish videos selected by the channel owner.

Token storage

Access and refresh tokens are intended to be stored locally on the channel owner's computer for workflow automation. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Video content

Uploaded videos, captions, hashtags, and metadata are provided by the channel owner and sent to TikTok only for the purpose of posting content.

Revoking access

TikTok access can be revoked through the user's TikTok account settings. Local tokens can also be deleted from the creator's local environment.

Contact

For questions about this private tool, contact the Speaki Cuayo channel owner through the official Speaki Cuayo social profiles.
